Global University Success: VGWS's dedicated University Placement Office has secured admissions to prestigious universities worldwide including Ivy League institutions, Oxford, Cambridge, IITs, and top universities in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, and India.

## Vishwashanti Gurukul World School Hosts Dr. Kiran Bedi as Chief Guest for Annual Day 2024

*Date: 2024*

Vishwashanti Gurukul World School, Pune celebrated its Annual Day 2024 with a display of talent, culture, and creativity, featuring Dr. Kiran Bedi as the chief guest. The programme commenced with a Welcome Address by Vice Principal Mr. Arpit Sharma, followed by the World Peace Prayer, the Lighting of the Lamp, and the Ganesh Vandana.

The Curtain Raiser, titled 'So Many Colours', was performed by EY1 and EY2 students. The Australian Chair Dance, Academic Toppers Reward, and Sports Achievement segment followed. Dr. Kiran Bedi addressed students on perseverance and the importance of education in shaping futures.

PYP 1 students performed a Hawaiian Dance; PYP 2 performed John Lennon's 'Imagine'. Cultural performances included an Indian Classical Group Song, a Tribute to Raj Kapoor (honouring the ideals of the school's founder, Professor Dr. Vishwanath Karad), a Maori Haka Dance, an Afro Jazz Dance, a drama on non-violence and peace, Russian and French Dance, and Japanese Dance.

The Annual School Report was presented by Head Boy Rohan George and Head Girl Shreya. Musical performances included a Drum Circle, Guitar, 'We Are the World', and a Fusion & Culmination performance. The event concluded with a Vote of Thanks by PYP Coordinator Ms. Dimpal Juneja and the National Anthem.

About Chief Guest Dr. Kiran Bedi: Dr. Kiran Bedi is a distinguished leader known for her pioneering work in public service as India's first female Indian Police Service (IPS) officer, a celebrated author and motivational speaker.

## Vishwashanti Gurukul World School Receives Official Authorization from IBO to Offer International Baccalaureate Career-related Programme

Vishwashanti Gurukul World School is proud to announce that it has officially received confirmation and authorization from the International Baccalaureate® Organisation (IBO) to offer the International Baccalaureate Career-related Programme (IBCP). This milestone reinforces the school's commitment to academic excellence, holistic development, and global education.

The IBCP is a framework designed to equip students with both academic knowledge and practical career skills. With this authorization, Vishwashanti Gurukul World School joins institutions worldwide dedicated to fostering critical thinking, ethical leadership, and lifelong learning.

The announcement was marked by a moment where the school principal, alongside the IBCP coordinator, displayed the official letter received from the IBO.

Speaking on this achievement, the Principal (Mr. Arpit Sharma) said: "We are deeply honoured to receive this authorization from the International Baccalaureate® Organisation. The International Baccalaureate Career-related Programme aligns perfectly with our vision of nurturing well-rounded, future-ready learners. This recognition reflects our commitment to providing a transformative education that empowers students to pursue their passions while building strong academic foundations. We are excited to embark on this new chapter of excellence."
