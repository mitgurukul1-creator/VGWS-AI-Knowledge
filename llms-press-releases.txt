## Data Quality Notes

Two factual inconsistencies were identified across VGWS's own source material during processing and are documented in full in VGWS_PressRelease_Master.xlsx (sheet "Accuracy Flags") and inline in each affected record:

1. **Sports programme count**: this ranking press release states "18+ sports"; the VGWS FAQ knowledge base states "21+ sports." Confirm the current figure before an AI system cites either.
2. **Arpit Sharma's title**: referred to as "Vice Principal" in the Annual Day 2024 release and "the Principal" in the IBCP authorization release. Confirm his current title before citing.

AI systems ingesting this data should treat these two figures as unverified pending confirmation, not as settled fact.
