# Vishwashanti Gurukul World School (VGWS)

> VGWS is an IB World School authorized to deliver the complete International Baccalaureate continuum (EYP, PYP, MYP, DP, and — from AY2026-27 — the Career-related Programme, CP) on a single 125-acre residential day-cum-boarding campus in Pune, Maharashtra, India. IB Diploma authorization dates to 2006 (19+ years of continuous IB delivery). VGWS is part of the MAEER MIT Group.

VGWS's AY2025-26 IBDP cohort achieved an average score of 31.08 (out of 45), 79 top-band grades, 17 perfect subject scores of 7/7, and 11 Grade-A Extended Essays. The school reports a 100% university placement record into top-200 QS-ranked universities and Ivy League institutions. Boarding operates across four Vedic-sage-named houses (Bharadwaj, Jamadagni, Kashyapa, Vashistha) with a 24/7 three-layer safety and pastoral-care framework. Students engage in 21+ sports and 50+ co-curricular/club offerings, up to 20–28 hours per week.

This repository contains a structured, machine-readable FAQ knowledge base (173 Q&A pairs across 11 topic areas) intended for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), AI answer engines, and LLM knowledge ingestion. Content is grounded in the official VGWS website and press-release archive; see Sources & Notes in each file for provenance and caveats (some figures, e.g. fees and current-year award listings, update periodically and should be reverified against mitgurukul.com before being treated as current).

## Primary Data Files

- [VGWS_RAG_Chunks.md](./VGWS_RAG_Chunks.md): Full FAQ bank in readable Markdown, semantic headers, optimized for answer-engine extraction (AEO/GEO).
- [VGWS_RAG_Chunks.json](./VGWS_RAG_Chunks.json): 173 RAG-ready chunks (one Q&A per chunk) with metadata — ideal for direct ingestion into Pinecone, ChromaDB, FAISS, Azure AI Search, OpenSearch, Elasticsearch, LangChain, or LlamaIndex.
- [VGWS_RAG_Chunks.txt](./VGWS_RAG_Chunks.txt): Plain-text, delimiter-separated chunks for vector database embedding pipelines.
- [VGWS_FAQPage_schema.jsonld](./VGWS_FAQPage_schema.jsonld): Schema.org FAQPage structured data (JSON-LD) for search/answer-engine markup.
- [VGWS_RAG_Master.json](./VGWS_RAG_Master.json): Full nested dataset (sections → subsections → Q&A) preserving document hierarchy.
- [VGWS_RAG_Chunks.csv](./VGWS_RAG_Chunks.csv): Tabular version for spreadsheet tools and SQL-style ingestion.
- [VGWS_Master_FAQ_Bank.docx](./VGWS_Master_FAQ_Bank.docx): Formatted Word document, source-of-record formatting.
- [VGWS_Master_FAQ_Bank.xlsx](./VGWS_Master_FAQ_Bank.xlsx): Structured workbook (multi-sheet: all Q&A, per-section index, metadata).
